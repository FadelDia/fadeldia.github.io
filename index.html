💡 What Is skills?

skills is a collection of small, focused abilities you can add to an AI assistant so it can do real work instead of just talking about it. Think of your AI assistant as a brand-new employee who is very smart but has never been shown how your team does things. This collection is the training manual — a set of ready-made "skills" that teach the assistant how to handle engineering and design tasks properly.

Each skill is a self-contained instruction set. There are skills for writing and reviewing code, skills for structuring design work, skills for building smooth animations, and skills for keeping every output consistent with the rest. Because they are packaged as plug-ins, you can add the ones you want and ignore the ones you do not.

The project is open, free, and built for people who want better results from their AI tools without spending weeks writing custom prompts. You download it once, switch on the skills you need, and your assistant gets noticeably more useful.

✨ What You Can Do With It

Engineering skills

Teach your assistant to plan a change before making it, follow consistent code style, explain errors in plain language, and check its own work before handing it over.

Design skills

Give your assistant a sense of layout, spacing, colour harmony, and typography so the things it produces look intentional rather than random.

Animation skills

Add polish to screens and prototypes with guidance on timing, easing, and motion that feels natural instead of distracting.

Plug-in convenience

Everything is organised as a plug-in collection, which means you can turn individual skills on or off to match the job in front of you.

🖥️ What You Need Before You Begin

You do not need to be technical to complete this guide. You only need a few ordinary things in place first:

  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer with an internet connection.
  • A modern web browser such as Edge, Chrome, or Firefox.
  • Enough free disk space for a small folder of text files — a few megabytes is plenty.
  • A free GitHub account is helpful for keeping track of the download, but you can view the project without one.

  4. Locate the file. Press the Windows key, type Downloads, and open the folder. Your new file will be at the top.

That is the whole setup on the Windows side. There is no installer wizard, no licence key, and no restart required.

🧭 Your First Five Minutes

Now that the folder is on your computer, here is a calm way to get oriented.

  1. Read the top-level README file. Open the folder and look for a file named README. Double-click it to open in your browser. It is the project's own short introduction.
  2. Browse the skill folders. Each folder represents one skill. Open a couple and read the plain-language description at the top of each file. You will quickly see which ones matter to you.
  3. Switch on one skill first. Pick the single skill most relevant to your work rather than turning everything on at once. It is much easier to judge a change when you make one at a time.
  4. Try a real task. Ask your AI assistant to do something you would normally do, and notice whether the output follows the new instructions more closely.
  5. Add more as you go. Once you are comfortable, enable another skill. Repeat until your set matches the way you actually work.

🔧 Keeping It Up to Date

New skills and improvements are added over time. To stay current, revisit the download page every so often and follow the same steps again. Because the project is made of simple text files, replacing your old folder with a fresh copy is safe and quick — just keep a note of which skills you had switched on so you can re-enable them.

A good habit is to update at the start of a new project rather than in the middle of one. That way nothing shifts underneath work you are already finishing.

🆘 Troubleshooting

The page will not load

Check that you are connected to the internet and that the address is typed exactly as shown. If it still fails, try a different browser or a private window, which rules out a stale saved copy of the page.

The download does not start

Some browsers block downloads until you allow them. Look for a small icon in the address bar or a notification at the top of the window and choose to allow the download, then try the button again.

I cannot open the folder

Right-click the file and choose Extract All. Windows sometimes keeps compressed files locked until they are extracted, and a normal folder will open with a simple double-click.

Nothing appears to change after I add a skill

Skills only take effect once your assistant picks up the new instructions. Close and reopen your assistant, or start a fresh conversation. Older conversations often keep the settings they began with.

❓ Common Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no cost, no trial period, and no account upgrade needed. You can use the whole collection without paying anything.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You need to be able to open a folder and read a short file. The skills themselves explain what to do, and this guide covers the rest.

Will this slow down my computer?

No. These are plain text instruction files rather than a background program. They use almost no space and do not run on their own.

Can I use only some of the skills?

Yes, and that is the recommended way to start. Each skill is independent, so you can use one, a handful, or the entire set depending on your needs.

How do I remove it later?

Delete the folder. That is all. There are no system files, registry entries, or background services to clean up.
