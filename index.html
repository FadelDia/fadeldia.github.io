AprilTag Guide

AprilTags are visual markers with unique IDs - similar to a QR code. A robot’s vision software can use a detected tag, along with camera and tag-size information, to estimate its position and orientation.

This guide is still being developed. For official instructions, check the resources provided by FIRST.
